SOLMAR

A sun-soaked open-world crime game

You are Rook, a driver with nothing but a borrowed jacket and a fast pair of hands. Dana, a fixer with a phone full of trouble, has four jobs for you — and Kessler, who owns the marina, is the man at the end of them. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar police, and take the city for yourself.

WASD — walk / drive  ·  Mouse — look around (click to lock)
E — enter / steal / exit a car  ·  Space — brake  ·  Shift — sprint
Q — draw / holster pistol  ·  Click — punch / shoot  ·  F — punch
P — pause  ·  M — mute sound

Progress saves to this browser automatically. An original game: every name, place and face in Solmar is invented, and no part of it comes from another game.
